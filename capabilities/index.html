Capabilities

Both sides of the gap.

Caladron works the mission side and the delivery side of defense personnel and readiness systems, and staffs them as one practice.

Mission and requirements

  • Requirements definition with the government customer.
  • Regulatory and business rules authorship.
  • Mobilization and force-generation process analysis.
  • Training, adoption and functional acceptance.

Delivery and technical

  • Technical product ownership.
  • Authorization and accreditation: Certificate to Field and ATO for cloud-hosted defense applications.
  • Secure multi-domain operations: NIPR and SIPR, cloud migration of legacy applications.
  • Legacy modernization.

Domain depth

Army MOBCOP and DAF TIMES and their supporting applications, the upstream personnel and readiness sources that feed them, and the downstream order-writing systems they hand off to. Reserve Component mobilization, force generation and sourcing across the full range of duty statuses and activation authorities.

One requirement, two processes. Caladron works both.
