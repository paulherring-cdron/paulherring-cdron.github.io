Founder and principal

Paul G. Herring

Paul G. Herring founded Caladron after sixteen years building and running the systems that mobilize the Reserve Component. He authored Tour of Duty for Army MOBCOP and led its development team for a decade, merging the legacy DAMPS-A application into it and changing how the Army Reserve and National Guard hire.

He then became the functional and product lead for a Department of the Air Force TIMES application suite across NIPR and SIPR, the first cloud-hosted suite at his firm: leading Certificate to Field and ATO, running requirements discovery with Headquarters Air Force A3, designing the applications, directing sprints and approving releases, managing cloud architects and developers, and writing the business rules, courseware and training himself.

He is a U.S. Army veteran, service-disabled. He holds an active security clearance.

Outside work he is a Brazilian jiu-jitsu black belt and coaches at a gym in Lorton.

Caladron LLC was formed in 2026. The experience above is the founder’s, offered as key personnel experience rather than corporate past performance.
